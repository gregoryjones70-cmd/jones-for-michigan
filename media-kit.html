At a Glance

Gregory Charles Jones is a disabled U.S. veteran (50% VA rating) living with lung cancer, based in Troy, Michigan. For two years he has been fighting to reunite with his three children -- Bilal, Jameel, and Maryam -- after Wayne County Circuit Court Judge Yvonna C. Abraham ordered a raid on his home on December 20, 2024, removing all three children and placing them with the same two people the children had just watched commit a strangulation.

Since then, Jones has documented thirteen motions denied, terminated, or suspended by Judge Abraham; filed federal civil-rights lawsuits; and filed a formal complaint with the Michigan Judicial Tenure Commission. Multiple licensed clinicians have independently documented that his daughter Maryam, 12, experienced suicidal ideation specifically tied to the prospect of being placed with her mother. He has not seen or heard his son Jameel's voice in approximately a year and a half.

Jones has announced independent, self-funded advocacy campaigns -- for the 2028 Third Circuit Court race and the 2030 Michigan gubernatorial race -- as a platform to bring public attention to the case, not as a conventional political bid. He is not a licensed attorney and cannot appear on a judicial ballot under Michigan law.

Key Facts

  • Three children involved Bilal, Jameel, and Maryam Jones -- all present for or affected by the events described on this site.
  • Presiding judge Hon. Yvonna C. Abraham, Third Circuit Court, Wayne County, Michigan.
  • December 20, 2024 Wayne County Sheriff's deputies raided Jones's Oakland County home on Judge Abraham's order and removed all three children.
  • ~18 months with no contact Jones has not seen or heard the voice of his son Jameel since the December 2024 raid.
  • 13 motions denied, terminated, or suspended Includes a Personal Protection Order for Jameel that Judge Abraham terminated the day before the raid.
  • Documented suicide risk Multiple independent, licensed clinicians documented suicidal ideation in Maryam, 12, tied specifically to the possibility of custody transfer to her mother.
  • Substantiated CPS finding Michigan CPS substantiated, at HIGH risk level, that Bilal was strangled by his mother in 2020; she was placed on the state's Central Registry for Child Abuse and Neglect.
  • Federal lawsuits and a judicial complaint Jones has filed federal civil-rights lawsuits and a formal complaint with the Michigan Judicial Tenure Commission.

Key Dates

2020 Bilal is strangled by his mother, Tenisha Hampton. Michigan CPS substantiates the abuse; Hampton is placed on the state's Central Registry for Child Abuse and Neglect.
June 8, 2024 Bilal is strangled a second time, by Hampton's boyfriend Douglas McClain, while Hampton is in the room. Jameel and Maryam witness it.
December 19, 2024 Judge Abraham terminates Jameel's Personal Protection Order against McClain, ruling Jameel "is not the alleged victim" of the strangulation he watched.
December 20, 2024 Judge Abraham orders a raid on Jones's home. All three children are removed and placed with McClain and Hampton. Jones is jailed roughly six weeks without trial.
August 21, 2025 Maryam's therapist documents suicidal ideation tied specifically to living with her mother, and none since living with her father.
December 1, 2025 Judge Abraham suspends Jones's parenting time indefinitely and authorizes law enforcement to retrieve Maryam, entered while Jones was involuntarily hospitalized and unable to appear.
March 3, 2026 The Michigan Court of Appeals dismisses Jones's appeal after Wayne County failed to produce the required hearing transcript.
